# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not report exploitable vulnerabilities in public issues. Email security@tinlance.com with a concise description, affected repository/path, impact, reproduction steps and a safe contact method. Do not include real customer data, credentials or private keys in the initial report.

## Handling

Reports are triaged privately. Please allow time for validation, remediation and coordinated disclosure before publishing details. Do not test against systems or data you do not own or have explicit authorization to assess.

## Product release policy

A scaffold or a successful build is not evidence that a product is secure. Security status must remain not_started, pending or failed until applicable scans and product-specific review provide recorded evidence. Regulated or high-impact products require separate threat models and specialist review.
